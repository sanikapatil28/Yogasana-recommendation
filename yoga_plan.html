{% if asana == "Tadasana" %}

📋 How to Do

  1. Stand upright with your feet together.
  2. Keep your spine straight and shoulders relaxed.
  3. Distribute your weight evenly on both feet.
  4. Keep your arms beside your body.
  5. Breathe normally and hold the position comfortably.

✅ Benefits

  • Improves posture and body alignment.
  • Helps improve balance.
  • Strengthens the legs and ankles.
  • Improves body awareness.

⚠️ Precautions

  • Avoid if you feel dizzy or unstable.
  • Use support if you have balance difficulties.
  • Do not lock your knees.
{% elif asana == "Vrikshasana" %}

📋 How to Do

  1. Stand straight with your feet together.
  2. Shift your weight onto one leg.
  3. Place the sole of the other foot against the inner leg.
  4. Bring your palms together in front of your chest.
  5. Maintain balance while breathing normally.

✅ Benefits

  • Improves balance and stability.
  • Strengthens the legs.
  • Improves concentration.
  • Encourages better posture.

⚠️ Precautions

  • Do not place your foot directly on the knee.
  • Use wall support if necessary.
  • People with knee problems should practice carefully.
{% elif asana == "Trikonasana" %}

📋 How to Do

  1. Stand with your feet comfortably wide apart.
  2. Turn one foot outward.
  3. Extend your arms parallel to the floor.
  4. Reach forward and lower one hand toward the leg or floor.
  5. Extend the opposite arm upward and breathe normally.

✅ Benefits

  • Strengthens the legs and core.
  • Engages multiple muscle groups.
  • Supports flexibility of the hips and spine.
  • Can be included in an active weight-management routine.

⚠️ Precautions

  • Do not force the stretch.
  • Keep movements controlled.
  • Stop if you experience pain or dizziness.
{% elif asana == "Bhujangasana" %}

📋 How to Do

  1. Lie on your stomach with your legs extended.
  2. Place your palms beside your chest.
  3. Press gently through your hands.
  4. Lift your chest while keeping the lower body grounded.
  5. Keep your shoulders relaxed and breathe normally.

✅ Benefits

  • Strengthens the back muscles.
  • Encourages spinal mobility.
  • Opens the chest and shoulders.
  • Engages the abdominal region.

⚠️ Precautions

  • Avoid with significant back pain.
  • Do not forcefully lift the chest.
  • Generally avoid during pregnancy unless cleared by a qualified professional.
{% elif asana == "Balasana" %}

📋 How to Do

  1. Kneel comfortably on the floor.
  2. Bring your hips toward your heels.
  3. Fold your upper body forward.
  4. Extend your arms forward or keep them beside your body.
  5. Relax and breathe slowly.

✅ Benefits

  • Encourages relaxation.
  • Provides a gentle stretch for the back and hips.
  • Can be used as a resting pose.
  • Promotes calm breathing.

⚠️ Precautions

  • Use a cushion if your knees are uncomfortable.
  • Avoid deep forward folding if it causes discomfort.
  • Practice gently if you have knee problems.
{% elif asana == "Utkatasana" %}

📋 How to Do

  1. Stand upright with your feet together or comfortably apart.
  2. Bend your knees as if sitting into a chair.
  3. Raise your arms overhead.
  4. Keep your chest lifted and spine long.
  5. Hold while breathing steadily.

✅ Benefits

  • Strengthens the legs and hips.
  • Engages the core.
  • Builds muscular endurance.
  • Useful in an active weight-management routine.

⚠️ Precautions

  • Practice carefully with knee pain.
  • Do not allow the knees to collapse inward.
  • Avoid excessive depth if uncomfortable.
{% elif asana == "Setu Bandhasana" %}

📋 How to Do

  1. Lie on your back with knees bent.
  2. Keep your feet flat on the floor.
  3. Place your arms beside your body.
  4. Press through your feet and gently lift your hips.
  5. Hold comfortably and breathe normally.

✅ Benefits

  • Strengthens the legs and hips.
  • Engages the back and core.
  • Opens the chest.
  • Supports overall body strength and mobility.

⚠️ Precautions

  • Move into the pose gradually.
  • Avoid excessive neck pressure.
  • Stop if you experience pain.
{% elif asana == "Paschimottanasana" %}

📋 How to Do

  1. Sit with both legs extended forward.
  2. Keep your spine upright.
  3. Inhale and lengthen your torso.
  4. Exhale and gently fold forward from the hips.
  5. Reach toward your feet without forcing the stretch.

✅ Benefits

  • Stretches the back of the body.
  • Supports flexibility of the spine and legs.
  • Encourages controlled breathing.
  • Can help improve mobility.

⚠️ Precautions

  • Do not force your body toward the feet.
  • Keep the movement gentle and controlled.
  • Bend the knees slightly if needed.
{% elif asana == "Virabhadrasana I" %}

📋 How to Do

  1. Stand with your feet apart.
  2. Step one foot back and turn it slightly outward.
  3. Bend the front knee while keeping it aligned with the foot.
  4. Raise your arms overhead.
  5. Keep your torso upright and breathe steadily.

✅ Benefits

  • Strengthens the legs.
  • Engages the core and hips.
  • Improves muscular endurance.
  • Supports an active yoga routine.

⚠️ Precautions

  • Keep the front knee aligned with the ankle.
  • Do not force the stance wider than comfortable.
  • Stop if you experience joint pain.
{% elif asana == "Virabhadrasana II" %}

📋 How to Do

  1. Stand with your legs comfortably wide apart.
  2. Turn one foot outward.
  3. Bend the front knee while extending the back leg.
  4. Extend both arms horizontally.
  5. Look toward the front hand and breathe steadily.

✅ Benefits

  • Strengthens the legs and hips.
  • Engages the core.
  • Improves stability and balance.
  • Builds muscular endurance.

⚠️ Precautions

  • Keep the front knee aligned with the foot.
  • Avoid excessive knee bending.
  • Practice within a comfortable range.
{% elif asana == "Navasana" %}

📋 How to Do

  1. Sit with your knees bent and feet on the floor.
  2. Lean back slightly while keeping your spine long.
  3. Lift your feet from the floor.
  4. Extend your legs as comfortable.
  5. Extend your arms forward and maintain steady breathing.

✅ Benefits

  • Strengthens the abdominal muscles.
  • Engages the hip flexors and core.
  • Improves core stability.
  • Builds muscular endurance.

⚠️ Precautions

  • Practice carefully if you have back pain.
  • Keep the spine controlled rather than rounding excessively.
  • Avoid if the pose causes pain.
{% elif asana == "Dhanurasana" %}

📋 How to Do

  1. Lie on your stomach with your legs extended.
  2. Bend your knees and bring your heels toward your hips.
  3. Reach back and hold your ankles.
  4. Gently lift your chest and thighs.
  5. Hold comfortably while breathing steadily.

✅ Benefits

  • Strengthens the back and legs.
  • Engages the core and posterior body.
  • Opens the chest and shoulders.
  • Improves overall body mobility.

⚠️ Precautions

  • Avoid with significant back pain.
  • Do not force the backbend.
  • Generally avoid during pregnancy unless cleared by a qualified professional.
